Build a Moritz Klein 5-step sequencer

Introduction

I've been working on building a 5-step sequencer, whenever I have a free weeknight, sometimes on the weekend. I've mostly been working on it at the Fat Cat Fab Lab in the West Village. It's a fun place! I always meet someone working on something cool. It's very welcoming.

I followed Moritz Klein's design and fantastic resources. I slightly deviated from the design in that I didn't include a step-length modifier. Being somewhat new to electronics, as an exercise:

  • I used the reference material to understand the logic of the schematic.
  • Laying out the assembly I'll try to do by myself.

It took me a while to finish it. I took several long hiatuses. Soldering without a guide is pretty difficult.
